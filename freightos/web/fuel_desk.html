How the payoff works

Kamino can lend temporary USDC to close the fuel loan and release the SOL. An Orca sale then repays the temporary credit in the same transaction. The estimate includes a flash-fee allowance. Collateral presets quote sales in whole cents. A cash-budget check prices the exact USDC output across the two recorded Orca pools. The SOL price check assumes one USDC is worth one dollar. Network fees and temporary account deposits require separate SOL. The loan must still hold its collateral. Reprice at payoff and plan the next stop using the SOL you have left.
