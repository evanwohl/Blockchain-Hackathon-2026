What do Gemini, Kamino and Solana each do?

Gemini: recorded oil-market thresholds stress the modeled diesel budget. The conversion to a pump bill is a sample assumption. The payment decision checks six fuel budgets; it buys no oil contracts.

Kamino: the driver’s own USDC reserve supplies the approved fuel payment through a vault withdrawal.

Solana: signatures authorize the parties, the program binds the reserve transfer to the approved bill, and atomic execution settles the contributions together. The receipt verifier checks signed instructions, token balances and the settled program record.

Inspect the recorded Gemini settlement inputs
What has shipped, and what is still a model?

The public payment is finalized on Devnet. The interactive bill executes on a local Solana fork. Both use test tokens. Fee savings, freight dates, customer terms and diesel estimates are modeled; the synthetic trial measures neither customer traction nor investment returns.

The driver supplies reserve capital. FreightOS does not eliminate invoice default, vault or liquidity risk. Live broker approval, invoice ingestion and commodity order execution remain roadmap work.
