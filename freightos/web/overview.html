Price the options

FreightOS checks which funds arrive in time. It compares the cost of using your reserve, taking early invoice pay, and drawing on available credit or cash.

Approve the payment

Check the amount and the fuel company, then approve. You get a signed receipt for the reserve transfer. Pay any remaining amount separately and record it on the bill.

What happens onchain?

The demo deposits digital dollars into a Kamino reserve on a local copy of Solana. Its payment program checks the approved amount and recipient. The signed reserve receipt can be replayed for verification. Early invoice pay, bank credit, and outside payments are modeled or recorded separately.
